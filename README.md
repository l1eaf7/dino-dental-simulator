# Dino Dental Surgery 🦖🦷

A pixel-art dental surgery game with dinosaur patients, by Dr D Do.

Scale, drill, fill and extract before the jaw snaps shut. Watch the patient's patience, keep the numbing topped up without overdosing, dodge a restless tongue and the odd sneeze, and keep your Doogle rating above one star.

## Play

| | |
|---|---|
| English | [`index.html`](index.html) |
| Korean (한국어) | [`ko.html`](ko.html) |

Works in any modern browser on PC or phone. No install, no sign-up.

## The patients

| Nickname | Species | Jaw closes | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rexy | Tyrannosaurus rex | fast | high |
| Trudy | Triceratops | normal | slightly above normal |
| Velma | Velociraptor | very fast | above normal |
| Steggy | Stegosaurus | a little slow | below normal |
| Brian | Brachiosaurus | slowest | normal |

## In this repository

- `index.html`, `ko.html`: the current game (each a single self-contained file).
- `versions/`: earlier versions, frozen as they were: the Original, Classic and the Cat Dental Surgery side project.
- `sprites/`: the sprite sheet, every visual element in the current game.
- `music/`: recordings of the three songs: *Open Wide*, *Smooth Enamel* and *Root Canal Rush*.
- [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md): what changed in each version.
