# Changelog

Every change goes into both the English (`index.html`) and Korean (`ko.html`) games.

## 5.0 Living patients (14–16 Oct 2026)

**Faces and mouth**
- Play-area faces redrawn to match the chair portraits (frills, feathers, plates, brow bumps, eye colours).
- The jaw closes much further, with teeth meeting at the canines, so closing is easy to see on phones.
- Thicker blue outline and bluer haze when the mouth is numbed.

**Tongue**
- Restless tongue rooted in the floor of the mouth: it feels its way round lower teeth and prods upper teeth twice.
- Only goes for teeth that still need a procedure; about 2 times in 3 it heads for the tooth nearest your pointer.
- Leans after your pointer at rest (up to one tooth's width), with a squish-and-spring idle blink.
- The tooth it is touching can't be treated until it moves away; tapping it costs patience.

**Sneeze**
- Touching the nose always makes the patient sneeze.
- "Ah… ah…": the upper lip flares to show more gum, the mouth stretches wide and the eyes half close.
- "ACHOO!": the lips purse into a thick ring over the teeth and the eyes squeeze shut; then the mouth opens back to where it was.
- The combo breaks, but a quick player can keep working through the "ah".

**Calculus**
- Redrawn as a crusty collar around the neck of the tooth that bulges past its sides; scaling exposes the clean tooth.
- From the second patient, calculus can hide caries (the cavity's edge peeks out) or a decayed tooth (the brown tip peeks out).
- Scaling over a decayed tooth hurts unless the patient is numbed, then it needs extracting.

**Chair patients**
- Tails 25% longer and fatter, wagging in choppy pixel steps (faster when happy or in pain).
- A 1-pixel idle bob.

**Game flow**
- Doogle reviews arrive on the score screen instead of mid-visit (fixes double reviews on phones).
- Walk-out mechanic turned off; mistakes cost stars on the review instead.
- "Special patient" renamed "patient quirk".
- On PC the Dental Supply store opens as a pop-up in the middle of the screen and pauses play.

**Sound and music**
- Higher, poppier extraction sound.
- Three songs: *Open Wide* (the original marimba loop), *Smooth Enamel* (acid jazz) and *Root Canal Rush* (a tense operation track, now quieter). Recordings are in `music/`.

**Tools**
- Debug panel on a button at all times: start level, money, background song, choose the patient (Rexy, Trudy, Velma, Steggy, Brian), tongue tour and an all-conditions test patient.
- Sprite sheet page (`sprites/`) showing every sprite and visual element in the game.

## 4.1 Polish (13 Oct 2026)

- Gold `+$` and red `−$` money floats in bold pixel digits.
- Seconds left on jaw and numbing, patience as a percentage.
- Game over at −$2,500 debt or a 1-star Doogle rating.
- Compact tutorial tips.
- Gloved-hand tools in the chair portrait (syringe, scaler, drill, filler, forceps).
- Debug test patient with every condition.
